# 🎬 Glimmerfall Animated Series — Google Flow Production Bible

> Designed for scriptwriting, episode storyboarding, and prompt generation using **Google Flow** and modern AI video tools.

## 🌟 Series Concept & World Lore
- **Title**: *Glimmerfall: Chronicles of the Nexus*
- **Setting**: The shattered continent of Glimmerfall, where four ancient civilizations struggle to restore the Grand Nexus Spire.
- **Format**: 10-episode animated fantasy action series.

## 🎨 Google Flow Visual Style Guide
- **Aesthetic**: Arcane dark-fantasy meets Studio Fortiche (Arcane style) with cinematic lighting, dynamic particle embers, volumetric god rays, and hyper-detailed stylized character designs.
- **Camera Language**: Sweeping aerial orbit shots, dramatic rack focus, tight profile parry cuts, high-altitude flight mount chase sequences.

## 🎞️ Episode 1 Treatment: *"The Glimmerfall Awakens"*

### Scene 1: The Shattered Spire (Opening Teaser)
- **Setting**: Dawn over the Sunspire mountain ridge. The floating Aether islands drift in mist.
- **Action**: A Solari Charger rides a golden warhorse across the canyon highway. Above, an Emberwing Hawk swoops down.
- **Google Flow Prompt**:
  ```text
  Cinematic anime dark fantasy establishing shot, soaring mountain canyon, floating crystalline spire glowing with golden radiant energy, a Solari knight in gilded heavy armor riding through mist, volumetric dawn sunlight, hyper-detailed fantasy art, 4k cinematic render --ar 16:9 --style raw
  ```

### Scene 2: The Umbri Ambush (Flash Spell Clash)
- **Action**: Shadows ripple across the stone floor. An Umbri assassin activates *Blinding Light* / shadow step, lunging forward.
- **Google Flow Prompt**:
  ```text
  Intense dynamic combat close-up, dark rogue shrouded in purple void smoke dodging with acrobatic precision, dual radiant blade parry creating golden spark burst, motion blur, anime action sequence, cinematic 60fps anime style --ar 16:9
  ```

### Scene 3: The Rite of Radiant Strike
- **Action**: Dawnbreaker Paladin channels *Radiant Strike*, hammering his mace into the earth to repel the void incursion.
- **Google Flow Prompt**:
  ```text
  Epic fantasy magic climax, armored holy paladin striking ground with incandescent golden warhammer, shockwave of solar fire and glowing runic glyphs exploding outward, floating dust and glowing embers, dramatic low angle shot --ar 16:9
  ```
