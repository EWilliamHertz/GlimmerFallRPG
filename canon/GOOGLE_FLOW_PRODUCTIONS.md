## 🌟 Series Overview: *Glimmerfall — Echoes of the First Shatter*

### The Narrative Arc
When the cosmic **Nexus Spire** fractured during the celestial cataclysm known as the **Glimmerfall**, the four fundamental elements of reality diverged into warring territories:
1. **The Solari Sun-Dunes**: Guided by radiant solar fire, strict honor, and celestial seraphs.
2. **The Terra Rootdeep Wilds**: Ancient primeval groves defended by towering stone colossi, druids, and primeval beasts.
3. **The Umbri Duskveil Undercroft**: A shadow-cloaked abyss ruled by ruthless bloodmages, assassins, and void abominations.
4. **The Aether Floating Isles**: Shifting crystalline archipelagoes where chronomancers manipulate time, space, and starlight.

The war centers around the gathering of ancient **Relics** (*Crest of the Sun*, *Heart of the Forest*, *Void Prism*, *Mirror Shield*). Whichever faction sockets three master relics into the Nexus Spire will dictate the cosmic fate of the next age.
