## ⚔️ Card-to-RPG Ability & Entity Translation Rules

1. **Entities (60)**:
   - In the TCG: Units deployed to the battlefield with Power and Health.
   - In the MMORPG: 3D NPCs, world mobs, companions, or dungeon bosses with stats scaled to RPG level.
   - In the TV Series: Named characters, combatants, soldiers, and creatures.

2. **Rites (33)**:
   - In the TCG: Main-phase tactical sorceries and grand magic.
   - In the MMORPG: Action bar combat abilities (cast-time or channeled high-potency spells).
   - In the TV Series: Climax spell sequences and visual VFX spectacles.

3. **Flash (15)**:
   - In the TCG: Instant-speed counter-spells and reactive tactical maneuvers.
   - In the MMORPG: Off-GCD interrupts, defensive shields, panic heals, and mobility blinks.
   - In the TV Series: Split-second parries, surprise reversals, and clutch tactical reactions.

4. **Relics (14)**:
   - In the TCG: Persistent artifacts providing continuous passive advantages.
   - In the MMORPG: The 3-card socketed Relic Deck in the `[C]` menu modifying stats & traits.
   - In the TV Series: Ancient artifacts of power fought over across the biomes.
